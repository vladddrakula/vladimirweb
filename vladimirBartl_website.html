Selected Thinking05

On clarity,
people,
and decisions.

Short observations from years of working in high-pressure environments, managing people, closing deals, and thinking carefully about how humans actually make decisions.
On Sales
"Objections are questions in disguise."
When a prospect pushes back, they're not saying no. They're saying they don't understand something yet — usually the value, the risk, or the relevance. The best closers are the best listeners.
On Positioning
"If you're trying to talk to everyone, you're talking to no one."
Specificity is scary. It feels like you're leaving people out. But the alternative — vague, hedged, all-things-to-all-people messaging — reaches no one. The narrower the target, the deeper the resonance.
On Decisions
"Urgency is often confusion wearing a deadline."
Most decisions that feel urgent are really decisions that feel unclear. Slow down, name the actual choice, and the path becomes obvious. The pressure usually dissolves with it.
